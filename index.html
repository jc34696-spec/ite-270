How we make it

Mix

1. Mix

We whisk yeast, flour, and warm sugar until the dough turns silky and happy.

2. Shape

Every ring is hand-cut and gently formed before the fryer sings.

3. Proof

The dough rests until it swells airy and light, ready for a golden finish.

4. Finish

Fresh glaze, bright toppings, and a box packed still warm from the tray.

🍩
